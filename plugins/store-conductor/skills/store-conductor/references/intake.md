# Client-need intake

What the conductor must know before it ranks anything. Ask in short rounds, plain language,
multiple choice where possible (the interactive question tool if available, otherwise a numbered
list answerable with a number), no more than four questions per round, and always allow "not
sure". If a question goes unanswered, write down the assumption you are using and continue;
never block on it. Re-plans only ask what changed since the last plan.

If `STORE-PLAN.md` exists, start from its section 1 and ask only to confirm or change it.

## Round 1: the business

1. **What kind of business is it?** Product store, appointment or service business, or both.
   This decides which page checks and which lanes apply.
2. **What are the top goals right now?** Pick up to three and rank them: more bookings or
   enquiries, more product sales, promote an offer or launch, build trust, grow search traffic,
   fix what looks broken, tidy tracking. For each, a number if one exists ("bookings per week").
3. **What must not change?** Regulated or medical claims, tracked booking or checkout links,
   brand look, anything contractual. These become hard limits that veto rows.
4. **Who approves, and how fast?** Name the approver(s) and the usual turnaround. Approval is
   needed at batch boundaries and always before anything goes live.

## Round 2: capacity and calendar

5. **How much can be done?** An effort budget: batches per month or hours per week on the
   client's side for review. This caps batch size and cadence.
6. **What dates matter?** Launches, seasons, sales, events, an agency handover. A near date
   promotes anything that serves it and defers risky work close to it.
7. **How cautious should we be?** Standard, careful (every claim fact-checked, smaller batches),
   or fast (larger batches, fewer pauses). Never "skip verification" or "skip approval to go
   live"; say so if asked.
8. **How often should the plan be refreshed?** Monthly, quarterly, or after each audit.

## Round 3: the store

9. **Which theme is the live one, and which is the draft or preview we work on?** Record both
   IDs and names. Nothing is pushed to the live one without an explicit yes.
10. **Who owns what the theme can't change?** Store settings (consent banner, checkout,
    shipping), apps and widgets, off-site pages (offers or booking sites on other domains).
    Name a contact for each so handed-off rows have somewhere to go.
11. **What evidence already exists and where?** Brand files, audit reports, an analytics
    export, a review export, Search Console access. Anything not shared is "not available".

## Echo-back

Write the answers back in three lines and get a yes before collecting evidence:

> Goals: 1) bookings, 2) trust, 3) tidy tracking. Must not change: tracked booking links and
> medical claims. Approver: Dana, two-day turnaround. Capacity: one batch of up to five rows a
> fortnight. Draft theme: 123 (unpublished); live: 456. Dates: spring launch on 1 March.

These go into section 1 of `STORE-PLAN.md`, with any assumptions marked "assumed".
