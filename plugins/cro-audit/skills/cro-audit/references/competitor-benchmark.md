# Competitive review (stream 3)

Visitors compare. Look at how others solve the same journey, from the public pages, to find what
shoppers expect and where this store is behind or ahead. Tag `[Heuristic]` (an observation about
another site is judgement, not data about this store).

## Which competitors

Use the competitors in `COMPETITORS.md` if it exists (from the `competitor-research` skill) and the
inspiration brands in `BRAND.md`. Otherwise ask for two to four, including one the person admires and one a customer
would realistically compare with. Say the choice came from the person.

## Compare the same journey

For each, load the homepage and the page that matches the audited page, at desktop and phone, and
record:

| Item | What to note |
|---|---|
| First screen | The headline, the primary action and its label, any overlay |
| Path to convert | Clicks and choices from landing to the conversion; whether price or time is shown on the way |
| Price visibility | Shown, "from" price, range, or hidden until later |
| Trust | Review count and source, credentials, guarantees, press, real photos |
| Offer | Free consultation, trial, bundle, membership, first-order incentive, and how it is framed |
| Friction | Account required, number of form fields, steps in checkout or booking |
| Mobile | The main action in the header, sticky bars, tap size |
| Speed | A quick measure (`scripts/pagespeed.py`) if the quota allows |

## Reading it

- Look for **expected patterns** that this store lacks (everyone shows price; this store doesn't).
- Look for **differentiators** to keep (a feature competitors don't offer).
- **Don't copy.** A pattern working elsewhere is a hypothesis for this store, not a fix. Note the
  context (audience, price level) before borrowing.
- Don't judge claims on competitors' pages; just report what they say.
